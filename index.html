About

Hi, I'm John-Michael.

“Success is not final, failure is not fatal: it is the courage to continue that counts.” — Winston Churchill

I used to say I was passionate about “tech”, until I realised I was spreading myself too thin. Three areas stuck. Cloud: it started when my father asked me to store his photos and contacts safely, and I wanted to build that kind of tool myself. AI: watching such fast progress up close is genuinely fascinating. Cybersecurity: I was targeted by an attack when I was younger, and I've taken security seriously ever since.

I did my Licence in computer science at Université Sorbonne Paris Nord (two years of it as a double degree with mathematics) and I'm now doing a Master's in Computer & Network Systems at Université d'Évry Paris-Saclay. I went to secondary school in the UK, so I work comfortably in English and French.

At Computer City I diagnosed and repaired PCs and laptops: motherboards, drives, RAM, broken BIOSes, fresh Windows installs, plus firewall and VPN troubleshooting for customers. I still refurbish old laptops as a hobby (see below).

Outside computing: I played competitive chess in a club for three years, and these days I'm hooked on tabletop role-playing games, for both the storytelling and the number-crunching. When I visit my parents, I tend their vegetable garden with my father.
